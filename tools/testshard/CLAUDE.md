# tools/testshard

**`tools/testshard` runs a package as several processes of one compiled test binary** — `./e2e`, and `internal/venue` and `internal/web` under `-race` — balanced by each test's duration from the last run (`.testshard/`, gitignored). Processes rather than `t.Parallel`, because a process keeps its own globals: `t.Setenv`, `t.Chdir`, a swapped `os.Stdout`, package-var seams and a daemon stopped by SIGINT to its own pid all stay correct, where blanket `t.Parallel` was measured and reverted. What processes do NOT isolate is the docker daemon and the machine's clock: a test that counts containers must scope to its own `steps.pid` label, and a test that asserts wall clock must leave margin for a loaded machine (the shards make one) — widen the window, or make the outcome not depend on it, never retry.
